[A] A7 interpretation (documented in runs/exp/A7/PREREG.md §1, with rejected alternatives)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sources: roadmap step 8 "A7 等算力：给两组相同的计算量（帧数）预算"; plan todo 21 "A7 = 与 E3a
同帧数预算"; cross-review table A7 = "等算力/等帧数" vs the A5 baseline. Because E2/E3a already
share the identical 20,000-step (20.48 M frame) budget, a literal "same frames" re-run of E2
would duplicate todo 17. Chosen interpretation (maximally informative for the architecture
verdict): **A7 = E3a's exact CNN trunk (conv_channels=192, num_blocks=40) with
use_event_branch=false, trained from scratch for 20,000 optimizer steps under E3a's otherwise
bit-identical conditions.** This yields the decomposition E3a−A7 = event branch at identical CNN
and frames (+1,031,296 params), E2−A7 = CNN width (c=202 vs c=192), E3a−E2 = combined effect.
Rejected: (1) E2 re-run (duplicate, no new contrast); (2) longer training for wall-clock parity
(violates the 20k-step mandate; synthetic 1.65x fwd+bwd ratio is not the real loop ratio, and
would confound architecture with budget); (3) widening the CNN for per-step FLOP parity (E2
covers param parity; FLOP parity ill-defined here; breaks the single-variable CNN-trunk
contrast); (4) mirroring A8 (an event-only arm, not a CNN reference).
